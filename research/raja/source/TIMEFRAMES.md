## The short answers

1. **Zones:** found on the 4-hour, then **redrawn on every smaller chart** down to the 30-minute. A zone
   is a **thin band at the edge of the candle bodies**, where several candles closed, with the wicks
   left sticking through it. Not at the wick tips.
2. **Buyer or seller:** he does not get one fixed answer from the 4-hour. The 4-hour gives the nearest
   support below and resistance above and how far each target is. He is a buyer once a candle **closes
   above** the resistance, or once a support has formed in an up-move; a seller on the mirror image.
3. **Which close he waits for:** it changes with the setup. The 30-minute is his default
   ("better confirmation"). In the Oct 2026 trade it was a **1-hour close through the zone, then a
   15-minute close**, and he entered inside the following 15-minute candle. With heavy volume he looks
   only at the 15-minute. He also takes entries **before** a close ("impulse" or "dynamic" entries).
4. **Consolidation:** his favourite condition. Box the range, measure how far price can run each way,
   then wait for a candle to **break and close** outside.

One thing first, because everything else depends on it.

## 1. On which chart he draws zones, and how

**The order (Analysis).** Daily first, only to read what the candles are doing; no zone from it is
used for the entry. Then the 4-hour, 1-hour and 30-minute, moving the zones each time:

> "as you move down the time frames you have to adjust those zones" (`NOxfOi4EP_c` 0:12:09)

**What the 4-hour frame shows (0:03:05).** Two grey bands only: the nearest support under price and
the nearest resistance over it, each about 1 dollar thick. The resistance band is at about 2416.3 to
2417.0. It sits just above the **tops of the bodies** of the two candles at the swing high (a green
close and the next red open, both about 2415.9); their upper wicks go on through it to about 2419.
The support band is at about 2385.4 to 2386.7, just under the **bottoms of the bodies** of the two
candles at the low; their lower wicks go through it to about 2381.5. The look-back is short: about a
day and a half of 4-hour candles.

**"Adjusting" (0:03:45).** On the 1-hour, that same 4-hour support band now cuts across the 1-hour
bodies. So he redraws it lower, where the 1-hour candles actually closed.

**"Zone depth" is thickness, and the answer is thin (0:04:08 to 0:07:17).** He is asked whether the
zone should be thicker and says no:

> "you have to look to see where the candles are closing" (`NOxfOi4EP_c` 0:04:08)

The redrawn 1-hour band (frame 0:06:10) is about half a dollar thick at 2383.5, with a line at
2383.51. Four 1-hour candles in a row have their body bottoms on it while their wicks reach down to
about 2379 to 2381. He counts them: four candles rejected that price, so one candle **closing** below
it is the sell signal. His reason for thin: with a thick zone you cannot say whether a candle has
closed beyond it or not.

> "I'm not going to make the zone thick like this to cover the wick" (`NOxfOi4EP_c` 0:09:45)

**On the 30-minute (0:07:39 to 0:10:45).** The 1-hour wicks turn out to be whole candles on the
30-minute, so the zone moves again. The 30-minute resistance (frame 0:10:45) is a band at about 2396.8
to 2397.3 with a line at 2397.06, on the body tops of the two candles at the high; their wicks poke
above to about 2397.6. The 30-minute support (frame 0:11:40) is a short band at about 2389.2 to
2389.6 under the body bottoms of a red candle followed by a green one.

**The same thing in the Oct 2026 trade (Replay, frames 0:09:05 and 0:10:45).** One band about 0.8
dollars thick with a line at 4126.35 (it reads 4125.90 on the 4-hour view). On the 1-hour it lies
along the **body tops** of the candles of the previous day and that morning, with wicks above it up
to about 4132. A second band at about 4140 to 4141 marks where price was rejected the day before.

**What the wicks beyond a zone are for.** They are the target, not the level:

> "if one candle is closing below this area ... these wicks, they are not rejections, they become
> wick fills" (`NOxfOi4EP_c` 0:07:17, shortened)

## 2. What makes him a buyer or a seller on the 4-hour

**He does not fix one direction from the 4-hour.** In Analysis he prepares both trades from the same
chart: a sell if a candle closes below the support (2383 on the 1-hour, 2381 on the 30-minute) and a
buy if a candle closes above 2397. His own definition:

> "a trend is defined by where you think price is going to go based on the time frame you're looking
> at" (`NOxfOi4EP_c` 0:01:41)

**What he does take from the 4-hour:**

- **Where the room is.** On the 4-hour he sees price sitting in a range, measures a 200-pip target and
  a 75-pip target, and picks the near one: "the safest bet is going to be to target right over here"
  (0:03:24).
- **Whether the current 4-hour candle is breaking things.** In the Replay, his case for buying, in
  the order he gives it at 0:09:02 to 0:09:43: the 4-hour candle "has broken above the London open
  high", it is heading for the Tokyo and Sydney open levels, it has broken above the previous week,
  and it "has also created a support". From that: "there's a high probability for price to continue
  to push up". On the frame (0:09:22) the 4-hour chart shows only five or six candles and the single
  line at 4125.90; the session opens are small markers the platform draws.
- **Support and resistance as candle pairs.** A support is "a bullish candle forming after a bearish
  candle" (`dPhUyysPFMc` 0:01:44), on any timeframe from 30-minute up. Once a support has formed, the
  next candle is expected to go up "as long as it respects the previous candle low" (0:03:08).

**With the trend, and what a sell in an up-move needs (Trend).** In an up-move he waits for a
pullback and buys when a support forms: "the trend is bullish so I'm just going to look for a support
to form so I can take a buy" (`XAHyqVd5Fsw` 0:08:19). A sell there is counter-trend, and needs a
support to form **and be broken** first (0:08:40 to 0:09:00).

## 3. Which candle close he waits for

It varies. What the four videos show, setup by setup:

| Setup | What had to close | Where he entered | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Break of a zone, normal conditions | A **30-minute** candle beyond the zone | After that close | Analysis 0:09:04 to 0:11:27 |
| Break of a zone, then smaller structure (the Oct 2026 buy) | A **1-hour** candle above the zone, then a **15-minute** candle above the two before it | **Inside the next 15-minute candle**, on its dip back to the previous high | Replay 0:08:00 to 0:12:51 |
| Range with heavy volume | A **15-minute** candle outside the range | After that close | Consolidation 0:00:00 to 0:01:02 |
| Buy from a support inside the range (his live buy of 7 Aug 2024) | Two supports formed on the 30-minute; **no break of resistance** | Seconds after a 30-minute candle closed green; he calls it a "dynamic entry" | Analysis 0:08:20 to 0:08:55, 0:11:27 |
| Pullback in a trend | A **strong-bodied** candle in the trend direction after the pullback candles | After that close, stop under the previous candle | Trend 0:09:21 to 0:10:06 |
| Trend with no pullback | Nothing | "Impulse entry" as the candle breaks the wick to its left | Trend 0:06:35 to 0:07:17 |

In his words:

> "the 30 minute is still a better confirmation time frame" (`NOxfOi4EP_c` 0:09:04)

> "you need this candle to actually close above this area" (`NOxfOi4EP_c` 0:11:07)

Why a close and not a touch: an open candle that has poked through "can close back in the range"
(0:10:47). In Consolidation he points at two candles that were each bullish at one point before
closing otherwise (0:00:41).

> "The 1-hour candle closed above the zone, and as the next 1-hour candle broke above the high, we
> continued to push up." (`dPhUyysPFMc` 0:08:22)

> "30-minute candle is still open. We don't know where it's going to go." (`dPhUyysPFMc` 0:09:43)

> "there's a lot of volume. So, I'm going to only focus on the 15-minute time frame" (`_aj37AaInxE` 0:00:00)

**The pattern across them:** the bigger chart (1-hour or 30-minute) has to have closed through the
zone. The entry itself then comes from a smaller chart, the 15-minute when the market is moving fast,
and it may be taken inside a candle once that smaller chart has closed in his favour. The less volume,
the more he leans on the 30-minute close; the more volume, the smaller the chart.

He describes the two habits as two kinds of trader: one "waiting for the candle to close because they
are very conservative", the other who "has missed so many trades by waiting" and takes the impulse
entry when there is volume and a trend (`XAHyqVd5Fsw` 0:06:35).

## 4. What he says about consolidation

> "whenever price is consolidating that is the best price action that I want to see" (`_aj37AaInxE` 0:00:20)

His reason: "there's no confusion" (0:00:00). The steps, from the 85 seconds and the frame at 0:00:30:

1. **Box it.** Two thin lines, one along the top of the small candles and one along the bottom. On
   the frame the range is six or seven small 15-minute candles after a strong rise. *The prices on
   that frame are UNREADABLE (a phone-format video).*
2. **Measure both ways before anything happens.** He gives 88 pips for one side and 100 pips for
   below, and finds the far end from the 1-hour: "there's a wick all the way through left hand side"
   (0:00:20).
3. **Wait for the close.** "Break and close. That is the key phrase." (0:01:02)

From the other videos:

- A range between two zones on the 4-hour is what he sees first in Analysis; his response is to pick
  the nearer target (0:03:02).
- "whenever price is consolidating volume is going to be your weapon" (`XAHyqVd5Fsw` 0:02:24): a
  close outside the range counts only "provided there's volume in the market".
- Too little room is a reason to pass: if the space to the next resistance is "a 15 to 20 pip range",
  he does not buy inside it and waits for a close above (`XAHyqVd5Fsw` 0:05:33).
- In the candle-by-candle walk he refuses every small-bodied candle in the pullback, "because it's
  low volume", and acts on the first strong-bodied one (`XAHyqVd5Fsw` 0:09:21 to 0:10:06).
